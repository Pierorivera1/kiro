# PROVISIONAL TEAM DRAFT — Teacher copilot canvas

**Not an organizer-supplied CANVAS. The exact five official prompts are unconfirmed because the official kit has not been received.** The headings below are team working themes only; they must not be submitted as answers to invented official questions.

## 1. Problem and situation — team hypothesis

A public-school teacher completing a training module still needs to translate general ideas into a context-appropriate lesson and capture evidence of classroom application. Reformatting and scattered notes consume preparation time. This problem statement needs validation with teachers; no interviews or measured time savings are claimed.

## 2. Persona and primary job — team hypothesis

A teacher planning for a primary or multigrade classroom with ordinary materials and uneven connectivity during class. The teacher, not an automated agent, decides curricular relevance, language, timing and adaptations. The current prototype itself requires a connection to its server; it does not claim offline generation.

## 3. Core story — implemented local slice

Select a clearly fictional training module → provide classroom context → obtain an editable proposal → review/save a lesson → add a short anonymous text reflection to its portfolio. The UI separates Preparar clase, Revisar sesión and Portafolio. Real Bedrock integration is implemented but not account-tested; local demonstration is deterministic and labeled, never claimed as GenAI evidence.

## 4. Desired outcome and success signals — proposed, not measured

A teacher can reach a saved session and a linked reflection in no more than 16 evaluator actions, understand the source and generation mode, edit every instructional section, and recover their work after ordinary navigation. Future validation should measure preparation time, perceived relevance, teacher corrections and whether evidence informs the next class. Do not assert learning gains from a prototype.

## 5. Boundaries and next decision — provisional

No student identities, grades, photos or uploads. No generic chat, authentication, roles or messaging. Begin with fictional/public material and text evidence. Next: receive the official prompts and challenge constraints, validate the story with teachers, then verify the permitted AWS environment and model before any deployment. The business/operating model and official submission format remain open.
